One article, three ways

Reading first: the date is never missing, the rest of the apparatus is there but never in the way. Each draft opens on an 8,600-word feature; the picker in the corner switches to a 317-word news short, a German piece, a 7,300-word interview and two more.

  1. AThe Leaf

    A printed page. One column on limestone, everything else — date, byline, topic, the plate — pushed into the left margin. The calmest of the three, and the only one with a drop cap.

  2. BThe Reading Room

    Reading as a session you can set: paper on limestone, a spine that shows the article's own sections and how long is left, and three remembered settings — ground (day / paper / night), size, measure.

  3. CThe Threshold

    The article's own image held full height as an overture, then a warm tinted field with nothing in it but the text, set larger and looser than anywhere else on the site.

/blog/ · the collection page

Three thousand articles, three ways

WordPress's own posts index, rendered into Blocksy's archive canvas. None of the three uses a card.

  1. AThe Ledger

    A finding aid: fourteen years as one month-by-month index with dotted leaders, instant search, and a loupe that raises whichever line is under the pointer.

  2. BThe Drift

    The decade as one current down a spine. Entries are sized by the article's own length — features open out, daily notes stay a line — with the years' density as a rail you can steer by.

  3. CThe Broadsheet

    One month set as a front page — lead, second tier, topic columns in rules — and 134 back issues you can open. Read the front page of March 2020.

  1. JJasper, the design system

    Palette, type, space, motion and components — the same system the /people/ drafts use. These pages add no new colour and no new face.
