The Berlin Conference

Man Is Not a Wolf to Man

For four centuries statecraft has been built on Hobbes’ libel — that man is a wolf to man, and only fear can hold him. Berlin answered with the older, truer image: the human being as imago viva Dei, the living image of the Creator, made to reason and to love. These are the voices that made the case.

July 2025 Berlin, Germany · & online Strategy · development · a musical offering

Hobbes told the modern world that man, left free, is a wolf — and so the world built itself a cage and called it security. But a wolf writes no symphonies, discovers no principles, forgives no enemy. The evidence of what man actually is stood on this stage in Berlin: a diplomat who chose peace over advantage, a scientist who serves life, a chorus that turned an ode into an argument. To organize the world around the true image of the human being is not sentiment. It is the only realism that has ever built anything lasting.
