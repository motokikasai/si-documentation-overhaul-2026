A world citizen is not a citizen of nowhere. It is someone who loves their own nation the way one loves a member of a family — and who therefore refuses to purchase its safety with another’s ruin. For forty years this Institute has argued that such people exist in every country, on every side of every border, and that the only thing they have lacked is a place to stand together. This conference builds that place. We are not convening to describe the world. We are convening to become the movement that changes it.

The Arc of the Day

One day, four ascents

The programme is provisional — sessions and times are placeholders that firm up as speakers confirm. The thread lights as you descend.

  1. Session I

    The State of the World

    The strategic hour named plainly: where the danger stands, and where the opening is. The keynote and the first panel.

  2. Session II

    The Programme of Development

    The physical economy the century needs — fusion, water, rail, the World Land-Bridge — presented by the people building it.

  3. Session III

    The Voices of the Youth

    The rostrum handed, for an hour, to the people who will have to finish the argument — from campuses on five continents.

  4. Finale

    The Musical Offering

    As always: not a resolution read aloud but an ode sung. Beethoven’s setting of Schiller, at the tuning of the human voice, chorus standing.
