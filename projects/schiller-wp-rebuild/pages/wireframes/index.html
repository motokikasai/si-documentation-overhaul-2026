Five ways the page could be shaped

Round one was three arguments in one layout. These are five layouts, deliberately short: the hero has already spent five screens persuading, so everything here orients instead. Grey on purpose — judge the shape, not the styling. Open one and use the two switches in its header: Notes explains what you are looking at, and Schedule source: live / empty shows what the page says when nothing is scheduled.

Every perishable value is chipped: auto computed at render from the archive · as of … carries the date it was true · scheduled comes from the events source and has an empty state. Nothing on these pages is a number somebody must retype.
