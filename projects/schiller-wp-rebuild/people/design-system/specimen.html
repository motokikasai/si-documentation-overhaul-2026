Schiller Institute · Design system

Jasper

A limestone ground, Prussian-blue ink, pale jasper relief and one brass ring — the palette of the neoclassical portrait medallion, from the decades Schiller wrote in. Built to sit inside Blocksy: the palette and type are written into Blocksy’s own settings, and everything else is a thin layer of tokens that follow them.

Principles

  1. Colour does structure.Blue means “you can act here”. Brass marks one thing per view. Nothing is coloured to decorate.
  2. The circle is the only curve.Corners are 0–2px. Round is reserved for people (medallions) and for filters (chips), so a circle always means a person or a choice.
  3. Serif reads, sans signposts.Anything you read is Source Serif. Anything you use — nav, labels, counts, buttons — is Source Sans.
  4. One dark band per page.Night is an event. A page may have one; most have none.
  5. Motion settles, never bounces.One easing curve, three durations, all zero under reduced motion. Content is visible before any script runs.
  6. Work with Blocksy.Set it in the Customizer if Blocksy has a setting for it. Tokens only fill the gaps, and they alias Blocksy’s variables.

The dark band

Night is an event, not a theme.

Secondary text on night uses #A9BCD0 (9.0:1). There is deliberately no site-wide dark mode: Blocksy’s header and footer would stay light, and a half-dark page is worse than none.

Type

Two faces from one family of design, self-hosted (Latin, Latin Extended, Cyrillic). Source Serif 4’s optical-size axis draws a sturdy text cut at 17px and a fine, high-contrast display cut at 58px from one file.
