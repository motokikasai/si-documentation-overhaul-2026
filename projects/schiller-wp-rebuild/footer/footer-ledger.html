A page ends here

Everything above this line is a stand-in for whatever the visitor was actually reading — an article, a conference record, a person. The draft begins at the footer.

Three footers are drafted against the same design system and the same content model. Each answers the same two questions in a different order: what else is here, and why should I believe any of it. Use the strip at the bottom left to switch between them without losing your place.

Prototype. No live functionality in this round: no language actually switches, no form submits, no count is queried. Every link points at the URL the content model will really serve (/people/, /conferences/, /media/, /videos/, /library/, /statements/, /coverage/), and every number is sourced — see the note under the table.
