Site footer · six drafts · 2026-09-18

The same footer, six ways

All six sit on Night, use only Jasper's roles and components, carry the eight WPML languages, and point at the URLs the content model will really serve. Round two — D, E and F — is the current proposal; round one is kept below for reference.

Each frame is the real draft, scrolled to its footer. Open one full-size: D · E · F · A · B · C. The carousels in D and E stop when you point at them, and the only hover effect is the portrait taking its colour back.
